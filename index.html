01 — About

Practical IT, built to last

I’m an experienced system administrator with a focus on network design, server administration, virtualization, VoIP, and email infrastructure. My work is about the unglamorous things that keep a business running: clean routing, documented configs, tested backups, and mail that lands in the inbox instead of the spam folder.

Over the years I’ve supported schools, engineering firms, contact-centre teams, and remote clients — from physical cabling and switches to hypervisors, reverse proxies, and hosted phone systems. I like problems that can be measured, automated, and then left alone.
